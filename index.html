schemagraph demos

Interactive pages from schemagraph, a schema context engine for text-to-SQL. All examples use Spider 2.0-Lite task local008: “the given names of baseball players who have achieved the highest value of games played, runs, hits, and home runs, with their corresponding score values.”

How the graph picks the tables

Table retrieval, stage by stage Question words seed the graph, personalized PageRank spreads the activation, PPR and BM25F rankings fuse, anchors are chosen, join paths pull in bridge tables, and the budget cuts the sub-schema. No LLM, deterministic.
